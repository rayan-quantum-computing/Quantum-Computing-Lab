# Quantum Cryptography & Computing Lab

Self-study log. I am learning quantum computing and quantum cryptography on my own, without a supervisor.

## What is in here

Small exercises where I test one idea at a time, and small tools I try to build and actually finish. Every file opens with a header comment: the date, what I was testing, what I expected, and what actually came out. Attempts that failed stay in the repo. They are the ones I learn from.

## Projects

- **quantum_password.py** — A password generator using quantum measurements. I found a statistical bias in the first version, measured it, and fixed it. https://github.com/rayan-quantum-computing/quantum-password-

  Full write-up: https://rayan-quantum.hashnode.dev/

More projects will be added here as I finish them.

## Where I am now

- Single qubits, Hadamard gates, and measurement statistics.
- Built and optimized the quantum password generator above.
- Next step: Two-qubit gates and quantum entanglement.

## Where I am trying to get

Simulating quantum key distribution protocols (like BB84) and studying post-quantum encryption vulnerabilities. That is years of work away, and I am aware of the distance.
